SPANISH-SPEAKING REGIONS

One Spanish language
different regional customs

Grafía works with a profile for each region and adapts to its character—forms of address, vocabulary and local conventions. Regional variants are respected rather than flattened, and the source text is preserved.

ESSpain
es-ES

Informal plural address uses “vosotros,” and the vocabulary has its own features (ordenador, móvil).

MXMexico
es-MX

The largest Spanish-speaking population. “Ustedes” covers every plural, with its own vocabulary (computadora, celular).

ARArgentina
es-AR

Rioplatense voseo (“vos tenés”) is common, even in advertising and informal writing.

COColombia
es-CO

“Usted” is widely used, sometimes even between close friends; Colombian Spanish is often seen as clear and neutral.

CLChile
es-CL

Chile has its own informal voseo, distinct from Argentina’s, and a highly distinctive colloquial vocabulary.

PEPeru
es-PE

Spanish with Andean influence, including Quechua loanwords (palta, choclo).

419Latin America
es-419

A shared variant for several Latin American countries, favoring wording that avoids any single country’s vocabulary.
