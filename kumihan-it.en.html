01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜Equilibrio helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Italian content.

01 / TYPE
Èé

Italian typography

Accents in perché, caffè and È; quotation choices and legal apostrophe wrapping. it-IT and it-CH remain distinct from EU or Swiss institutional house styles. Numbers and dates keep their original values.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY
Aa/è

Source fidelity

Names, prices, figures, accents, quotations, code and original NFC/NFD bytes stay exactly as supplied.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.

ITALY / SWITZERLAND

One Italian language
different regional customs

Equilibrio works with a profile for each region and adapts to its character—vocabulary, the way numbers are written and local conventions. Regional variants are respected rather than flattened, and the source text is preserved.

ITItaly
it-IT

The reference standard Italian, with a decimal comma and a point for thousands (12.345,67).

CHSwitzerland
it-CH

An official language in Ticino and Italian-speaking Graubünden, with its own Swiss terms (azione, licenza di condurre) and figures like 12’345.67.
