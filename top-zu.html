Uma ulimi lushintsha,nokuhlelwa kombhalo kuyashintsha

I-KUMIHAN iyiphrojekthi yomthombo ovulekile esekela ukuhlelwa kombhalo okufanele ulimi ngalunye kanye nedizayini yakho konke okwakhiwe ngombhalo. Kumawebhusayithi nemibhalo, ihlela isakhiwo solwazi, izinhlobo zezinhlamvu, isikhala esingenalutho nokugeleza kokufunda, futhi ibuyekeza izakhiwo ezijwayelekile nemihlobiso engenanhloso evame emisebenzini eyenziwe yi-AI. Idizayini enenhloso, yalokho okushoyo nendlela okusho ngayo.

OUR APPROACH

Ngaphezu kokuhumushaIdizayini yokufunda okungcono

I-KUMIHAN ayisebenzi kuphela ngemigqa yezinhlamvu nokwehlukaniswa kwemigqa. Kunoma iyiphi idizayini lapho umbhalo uphethe khona ulwazi — amakhasi ewebhu, ama-athikili, imibiko, amaslayidi — icabangela ndawonye isakhiwo, izinhlobo zezinhlamvu, isikhala esingenalutho nokugeleza kokufunda.

Isigaba esifanayo esiphezulu, amakhadi afanayo nezimemo ezifanayo zokwenza isenzo njalo; izikhala ezigcwaliswe ngemihlobiso nje; impumelelo eboniswa ngaphandle kobufakazi. I-KUMIHAN inemithetho yokwakha neyokubuyekeza ukuze kucatshangwe kabusha ngale dizayini ejwayelekile ye-AI. Esikhundleni sokwenqabela ukubukeka okuthile, inikeza imihlahlandlela yokwahlulela ukuthi idizayini iyafanela yini okuqukethwe nenhloso yayo.

01Vikela umbhalo wokuqalaGcina amagama, izinombolo namazwi anikeziwe
02Hlela ngokolimi ngalunyeLungisa ukwehlukaniswa kwemigqa, izimpawu zokubhala, izinhlobo zezinhlamvu nesikhala
03Buza imikhuba ye-AINciphisa izakhiwo eziphindwa ngokwejwayelo nemihlobiso engenanhloso
04Hlola umphumela wangempelaHlola izikrini nokufundeka, futhi uveze okungakahlolwa
