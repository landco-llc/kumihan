01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜字衡 helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Traditional Chinese content.

01 / REGION
「」

Taiwan and Hong Kong in context

Quotation, punctuation, glyphs and spacing are interpreted in regional and editorial context. Taiwan conventions are not silently imposed on Hong Kong.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY
臺 / 港

Source fidelity

Names, brands, prices, URLs, regional variants and Unicode sequences are never normalized merely to fit a layout.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.

TAIWAN / HONG KONG

One writing system different editorial contexts

In both regions the supplied code points are preserved. Actual glyphs and line breaks still require inspection in the target renderer.

TWTaiwan
zh-Hant-TW

For newly written prose, 「」 and nested 『』 are an initial recommendation—not a mandate for supplied quotations or every publisher. Punctuation squeezing is optional.

HKHong Kong
zh-Hant-HK

First identify the publisher, institution or project house style for quotations, glyphs and punctuation. Taiwanese educational conventions are not the default.
