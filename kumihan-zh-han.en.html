01 / CONCEPT

Not another rigid template
Typography that follows your content

ZiHeng treats typography as part of information design. Decide who will read it, on what medium, and in which region before addressing punctuation, wrapping, type, rhythm and hierarchy. It does not impose a universal font pair or layout recipe.

02 / PRINCIPLES

Readability is built in the details

Guidance should be explainable, with room for editorial judgment.

01
「」

Taiwan and Hong Kong in context

Quotation, punctuation, glyphs and spacing are interpreted in regional and editorial context. Taiwan conventions are not silently imposed on Hong Kong.

02
臺 / 港

Preserve every supplied character

Names, brands, prices, URLs, regional variants and Unicode sequences are never normalized merely to fit a layout.

03
Aa 字

Design for the content

Adjust hierarchy, line lengths, spacing and fallback to the reader and actual medium—not to a pre-selected font palette.

04
✓

Evidence-based read-only review

Report static candidates, observed rendering and unchecked conditions separately. Reviews do not change the original file.

03 / LOCALE

One writing system different editorial contexts

In both regions the supplied code points are preserved. Actual glyphs and line breaks still require inspection in the target renderer.

TWTaiwan
zh-Hant-TW

For newly written prose, 「」 and nested 『』 are an initial recommendation—not a mandate for supplied quotations or every publisher. Punctuation squeezing is optional.

HKHong Kong
zh-Hant-HK

First identify the publisher, institution or project house style for quotations, glyphs and punctuation. Taiwanese educational conventions are not the default.
