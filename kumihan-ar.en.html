01 / CONTEXT

Typography starts with meaning

Direction is part of reading. Handle Arabic and mixed scripts in context without reversing characters or changing names and numbers to fit a layout.

02 / PRINCIPLES

Four priorities for readable text

01

Clear direction

Distinguish language, paragraph direction and alignment. Isolate Latin links and identifiers where their context calls for it.

02

Source integrity

Preserve diacritics, intentional Tatweel, invisible controls, names and prices exactly as supplied.

03

Context-led design

Choose hierarchy, spacing and available typefaces for the content and audience. A fixed font recipe cannot replace that choice.

04

Evidence-based review

Separate static diagnostics, observed browser rendering and editorial judgment. Unchecked results remain unchecked.

KUMIHAN / DESIGN

Room for the text and clarity for the reader

Every heading, paragraph and link serves the content. Do not add decoration to fill space or change Unicode order to repair display.
