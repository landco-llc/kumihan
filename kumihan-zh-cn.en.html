KUMIHAN｜字律 is an open-source set of skills and plugins for creating and reviewing Simplified Chinese websites, documents and presentations with AI. It focuses on punctuation, line breaks, mixed Chinese and Latin text, source preservation and evidence-based quality checks.

WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Typography starts
with the content

Converting to simplified characters does not make Chinese text readable on its own. Punctuation, line boundaries, spacing between Chinese and Latin text or numbers, and the glyphs of the chosen typeface all shape the reading experience. ZiLu helps AI decide how to set text by first asking what it needs to say and where it will be read.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Simplified Chinese content.

01 / TYPE
文

Punctuation & line breaks

Review meaning, punctuation and actual line boundaries at the target width. Keep proposed edits separate.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Mixed-script typography

Distinguish Chinese prose, Latin text, numbers and code. Adjust visual spacing without changing source characters.

03 / FIDELITY
Aa/字

Protect the source

Preserve names, brands, prices, numbers, quotations and approved copy.

04 / REVIEW
◎

Review with evidence

Distinguish static checks, rendered evidence and human judgment. Untested results never become passed.

DESIGNED FOR THE MESSAGE

Beyond arranging type
Design the reading experience

ZiLu does not impose a fixed typeface or layout. It adapts to the content, medium and brand, and treats source accuracy and usability as part of the design.
