WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜Soulad helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything. In Czech, that starts by asking whether a text is to be read at length, scanned, recognized at a glance, looked at as form, or acted on.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Czech content.

01 / TYPE
č ř ů

Czech typography

Čárka, háček and kroužek are part of the letter, not decoration: á and a, ů and ú, i and y are different spellings, and the capitals Č, Ř and Ů keep their marks. Ď and ť are single letters whose háček can look like an apostrophe—never d’ or t’. One-letter prepositions such as k, s, v and z are not left at the end of a line, and hyphenation follows Czech word structure, not English dictionaries.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal. Continuous reading, scanning, quick recognition, letters as image and interface labels are designed as different roles—as in Teige’s Abeceda (1926), where a poem to be read and large letters to be looked at share one book. English Title Case is not carried over to Czech headings.

03 / FIDELITY
„ “

Source fidelity

Protect names, quotations, numbers, dates, codes and intended meaning. Spaces carry meaning: 30 % is a quantity, 30% sleva an adjective; 18 °C takes a space, 45° does not; 1 250,50 Kč and 20. 10. 2026 follow Czech conventions, and both 8.05 and 8:05 are accepted. Quotation marks „…“ and ‚…‘ stay as written. Word order, case endings and ty/vy carry meaning, so changing them is an edit: recorded as a proposal, never made silently.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files. A typeface is checked for more than having the glyphs: háček and čárka in capitals, bold, italic and small sizes, close pairs such as ďá and ťe, and marks above capitals that tight headings can clip. Zoom, reflow and changed text spacing are checked in real browsers, and anything unverified is reported as unverified.

DESIGNED FOR THE MESSAGE

Beyond arranging type
Design the reading experience

KUMIHAN｜Soulad doesn't dictate a finished look. It gives AI the knowledge to adapt to each medium and brief. There is no single “Czech style”—red and black or condensed capitals are choices, not rules—and Czech text does not need a Czech-made typeface. Typefaces are compared by file version, character coverage, the shape of ď and ť, figures and licence.
