OPEN-SOURCE SKILLS / SIMPLIFIED CHINESE TYPOGRAPHY

Natural Simplified Chinese typography,
made with AI

KUMIHAN｜字律 is a set of open-source AI skills for creating and reviewing Simplified Chinese websites, articles, reports, and presentations—with care for readability, layout, and the integrity of your original content.

WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜字律 helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Simplified Chinese content.

01 / TYPE
文

Punctuation & line breaks

Review meaning, punctuation and actual line boundaries at the target width. Keep proposed edits separate.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY
Aa/字

Source fidelity

Preserve names, brands, prices, numbers, quotations and approved copy.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.
