01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜অক্ষরসজ্জা helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Bangla content.

01 / TYPE
ক্ষ

Bangla typography

Logical order, vowel signs, conjuncts, reph, line boxes and mixed scripts need actual font checks. Keep bn-BD and bn-IN editorial choices separate.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY

Source fidelity

Names, prices, digits, punctuation, ZWJ/ZWNJ and original code point order remain exactly as approved.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.

BANGLADESH / INDIA

One Bangla language
different regional customs

The skills work with a profile for each region and adapt to its character—spelling conventions, vocabulary, digits and currency. Regional variants are respected rather than flattened, and the source text is preserved.

BDBangladesh
bn-BD

Bangla is the state language; Bangla Academy standard spelling is widely followed, and the vocabulary has its own features (পানি for water).

INIndia
bn-IN

Spoken in West Bengal and Tripura; the vocabulary has its own features (জল for water), and spelling varies from publisher to publisher.
