01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜Mizani helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Swahili content.

01 / TYPE
ng’

Swahili typography

Protect ng’ and the original apostrophe characters, respect long words, and explicitly select locale formats for sw-TZ, sw-KE or sw-CD.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY
Aa/’

Source fidelity

Protect names, quotations, apostrophes, numbers, dates, codes and intended meaning. Record changes as proposals, never silent edits.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.

TANZANIA / KENYA / UGANDA / DR CONGO

One Swahili
different regional formats

Region is never inferred from “sw”: choose sw-TZ, sw-KE, sw-UG or sw-CD explicitly. Currency and time zone come from the brief, and the source text stays unchanged.

TZTanzania
sw-TZ

Numbers are formatted with Intl/CLDR for sw-TZ. Swahili time is labeled separately from civil time; actual event timestamps are never shifted six hours.

KEKenya
sw-KE

Use lang="sw-KE". Kenyan youth slang (Sheng) is not treated as a universal Swahili convention; approved brand copy is respected.

UGUganda
sw-UG

The sw-UG profile is selected explicitly. Currency is never inferred from language or region—it must be stated, and original amounts are kept.

CDDR Congo
sw-CD

sw-CD numbers and currency are formatted separately, not copied from another country’s pattern. Original amounts stay as supplied.
