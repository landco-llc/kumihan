WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜Zetwerk helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything. In Dutch, that starts with IJ, compounds, the trema and line breaks that can change how a word is spelled.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Dutch content.

01 / TYPE
IJë

Dutch typography

Capitalize word-initial IJ as a pair (IJsland), keep compounds such as langetermijnplanning whole, and tell the trema from the hyphen (poëzie, gala-avond). Hyphenation can change spelling (beëindigen → be- / eindigen), so line breaks are checked in the real target environment, not assumed.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY
Aa/’

Source fidelity

Protect names (Van den Bossche), apostrophes (oma’s, ’s Morgens), amounts (€ 5,00), deadlines (tot en met) and regional words such as bankkaart. Record changes as proposals, never silent edits.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.
