# Documents and slides

Use the host's real RTL paragraph and page controls; right alignment alone is insufficient. Keep Unicode source logical and preserve protected names, prices and references. Review mixed-script tables, bullets, page numbers, numeric columns and navigation. Compare available licensed fonts using the supplied text, marks and exported result; do not redistribute fonts.

After PDF/Office export, inspect shaping, mark clipping, fallback, line breaks, reading order, selection/copy, searchable text and editability in the actual host/version. Record page and example, screenshot or extracted evidence, exact export options and limits. A document playbook is guidance-only: Office, InDesign, Figma and PDF accessibility certification are not performed by this package.
