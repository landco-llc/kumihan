# Documents / Office / PDF / slides

Preserve editable masters and original bytes before extraction. Record application/version, complex-script font settings, actual font license/file/weight, page/slide size and output path. Prototype Hindi A4 body 11–12pt with 1.5–1.7 line height, then inspect actual matras, tables, page breaks and glyphs. Do not treat trial sizes as standards.

Inspect Office originals and final PDF separately. Check font embedding rights and actual embedded fonts; test search, selection, copy/paste and extraction order against the source. A visually correct PDF may extract incorrectly. Do not silently normalize extracted source or declare a new extractor result equal after dropping signs or spaces. If rasterizing/outlining, keep an accessible editable master and exact text correspondence. Image generators must not be trusted to typeset final Hindi. Mark unavailable application, PDF extraction or native reader checks NOT_CHECKED.

Slides and display use purpose-led hierarchy, concise supplied content and real typographic roles. Avoid country/script stereotypes and unexplained ornament; keep source quotations and new draft copy distinct.
