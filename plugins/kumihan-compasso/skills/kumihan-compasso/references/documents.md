# Documentos / Reports, presentations and PDF

- Keep article semantics, headings, sources, footnotes, tables, labels and real data bound to the original context.
- Use pt-BR / pt-PT and editorial guide as separate explicit choices. Verify big number words: bilhão, bilião, mil milhões must match source quantities.
- Avoid splitting numerals from measurements or currency marks. Space rules depend on authority and medium; never change unquoted originals by assumption.
- Check accents, quote glyphs, ordinals and fallback at actual output font sizes and weights.
- Test widow/orphan paragraphs, running heads, figure captions and reading order, plus PDF text extraction and assistive technologies when those environments are available.
- PowerPoint, Google Slides, Word, print and PDF each require real-render checks; source inspection is not proof of appearance.
- Maintain content-led editorial composition, not polished generic AI decoration.
