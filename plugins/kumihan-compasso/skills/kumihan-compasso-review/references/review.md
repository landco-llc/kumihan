# Revisão sem alteração / Non-destructive review

- Classify each issue as verified normative rule, house-style candidate, human editorial judgment, or rendered-only observation.
- For each issue include original text, context, affected medium, source ID, suggested remedy and risk. Explicitly distinguish NOT_CHECKED from PASS.
- Do not automatically rewrite provided content or metadata. Preserve encoding and byte-for-byte source when requested.
- Protect quoted passages, trademarks, URLs, identifiers, dates, numeric magnitude and units.
- Preserve legal and financial meaning; no automatic BR/PT lexicon replacement, currency conversion, punctuation modernization or inferred regional pronouns.
- Detect suspicious arbitrary line-break CSS, number–unit joins, missing diacritics only as advisory where semantics may vary.
- Test Portuguese typography separately from quality of general graphic design. Evaluate hierarchy and brand identity, not just text normalization.
