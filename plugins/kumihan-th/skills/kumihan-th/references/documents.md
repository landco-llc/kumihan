# เอกสารและงานนำเสนอ / Documents

Apply [workflow](workflow.md), report-document or presentation profile, retaining original source separately from DOCX/PPTX/PDF projection. Verify actual Office/PDF host, canvas/page size, font installation, embedding license, fallback and export path. Never claim export correctness from source markup alone.

Inspect headings, table cells, form fields, footnotes, references, Thai/Latin baseline, tone marks above/below, adjacent-line collisions, clipping and selection/copy of full source in the real output. Recheck after font fallback/export and enlargement. Record missing text and accessible full source where truncation is used. Preserve legal text and citations exactly.

Typed amounts, identifiers and original dates remain unchanged. Format locale, currency, numbering system, calendar (Gregorian/Buddhist), time zone and instant as separate projection fields. Ask about ambiguous dates; never infer a calendar or convert account numbers. Thai in vertical Japanese composition is guidance-only: inspect a sideways Thai run in the actual renderer and mark needs-review/not-checked until observed.
