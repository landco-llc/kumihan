# Contesto e integrità / Context and fidelity
Resolve language, locale (it-IT baseline only when explicitly selected; unknown stays unknown), medium, content_role, house_style, source_policy, audience, editorial_voice and evidence independently. it-CH is not a Swiss federal house style. Choose typesetting_only, proofread or rewrite; no silent content editing. Typesetting keeps original words and values. Proofreading proposes contextual corrections with permission; rewriting needs explicit scope. Review is always read-only regardless of mode.

Create a design contract: purpose, audience, principal information, reading order, typography roles, density, intended emphasis, responsive strategy, exceptions and evidence. Changes must identify rule ID, source reference, scope, before/after proposal, edit permission, status and observed environment. Distinguish normative language, institutional style, design proposal and measured behavior. Unknown grammar is needs-review; unmeasured rendering is not-checked.

Preserve é/è/È, perché/caffè, e/è, da/dà, si/sì, te/tè, qual è, po’ versus river Po, names, foreign text, quotations, URLs, JSON/JS/SQL, IDs, original NFC/NFD bytes, combining sequences, numeric precision, currencies, dates and time zones. Never normalize originals silently or reinterpret 1.234. Permit contextual alternatives se stesso/sé stesso, tu/Lei/voi and author-selected inclusive language. Original quotations override preferred spellings.

Examples: Qual’è il formato? → propose Qual è il formato? only in editable ordinary prose (R03/L01). Preserve «qual’è» in a spelling discussion. Un pò di spazio → propose po’ after confirming the intended abbreviation (R04/L03); keep Il Po. Do not regex-correct every e to è, every fa to fa’, or French café to caffè. These are guidance examples, not helper grammar detections.
