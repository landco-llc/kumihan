# Verification scope

This edition provides typography guidance and bounded checks. Static source-preservation or package checks do not establish native-host acceptance, complete language correctness, Office/PDF rendering, IME or screen-reader behavior. Verify the original text and final output in the intended application. Follow the product README for supported locales and media.

## Regional guidance in v0.1.2

Regional acceptance covers 58 parent candidates: 2 complete, 5 partial, and 51 with no executed coverage. Seven bounded assertions and five separate helper observations passed. These results do not establish all 58 candidates as implemented or tested.

Additional institutional profiles, vocabulary and design examples are reference-only. They do not add exact numeric/date formatting, mixed-content partial editing, native Office layout, native proofreading, legal certification, or full WCAG acceptance. Runtime observations require their actual locale, runtime and options; visual observations require the actual renderer, font and dimensions.

Package checks cover the 15 ZIP archives, checksums, included licenses and icon metadata. They do not establish successful installation or icon display in every ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini client.
