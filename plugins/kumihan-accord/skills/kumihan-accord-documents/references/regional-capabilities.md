# Regional capabilities / Capacidades regionales

## Select authority explicitly

Keep language, geographic audience, formatting locale, institutional house style,
register and medium separate. The helper retains its existing four locale inputs
and three executable punctuation styles. New institutional profiles are
reference-only; do not pass a reference ID to the helper as an executable style.
An omitted style is unresolved. A region, country address or unknown locale
never authorizes a France or EU fallback.

ULB concerns its adopted document typography. Swiss federal guidance keeps
four-digit grouping on hold and does not supply a complete punctuation table.
Vaud quantity apostrophes differ from federal grouping; excluded older electronic
media guidance must not become a new Web default. Luxembourg SIP is the third
revised January 2012 edition, conditional on publisher and document policy.
Do not infer numeric or punctuation capabilities from profile registration.

## Inspect, propose, format and render are different

`inspect` is read-only: retain exact UTF-8 source and report candidate ranges.
The plain-text helper evaluates only selected punctuation and guillemet gaps.
OQLF accepts zero and fine nonbreaking gaps before `! ? ;`; U+202F is the
product's code-point realization of the fine form. Do not normalize an accepted
form merely to make every string identical. Explicit proposals may normalize
nonconforming gaps under their selected policy, and remain previews.

A proposal requires authorized generated one-line plain text. URL/code/markup
refusal protects the entire proposed input; it does not imply partial editing of
mixed content. Never flatten an HTML, Markdown, DOCX or PDF document through
this helper. Unknown language spans, attributes, source-bound protected ranges,
CSS-generated quotes and typed values require a compatible structural adapter.
An opt-in context adapter, if present, remains limited to its declared input
contract; preserving unsupported fields does not execute their semantics.

No exact decimal/currency/date formatter is provided. Preserve amount, explicit
currency, precision, rounding authorization, date kind and timezone separately
from display. Do not infer currency from locale or replay old Intl output as a
universal expected string. Runtime observations need actual runtime/options;
visual evidence also needs renderer, font and dimensions.

## Meaning and display references

Regional number words, meal names, mobile labels and `tu`/`vous` are
meaning/register references. Preserve approved names such as NATEL and mixed
language brands. Review audience and editorial voice; do not automatically
replace valid regional vocabulary or use a single illustrative copy as an oracle.
Organization-specific designs are references, not country font/color defaults.
Asset/font availability is separate from redistribution permission.

Keep ordinary Web text, protected existing logos and newly approved graphical
copy distinct. Graphic output needs original text/asset provenance and actual
glyph review. Static Unicode checks do not establish line breaking, copy and
selection, bidi behavior, glyph coverage, WCAG, legal compliance, native Office
or print acceptance. Report each unexecuted scope as not-checked.
