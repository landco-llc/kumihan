WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜Szyk helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Polish content.

01 / TYPE
ŁŻ

Polish typography

Polish diacritics, one-letter words at line endings, local number formats and quotation marks. Address forms and audience come from the brief, not assumptions.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY
Aa/Ł

Source fidelity

Keep original names, prices, numerical values, Polish diacritics and the exact source text intact.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.

DESIGNED FOR THE MESSAGE

Beyond arranging type
Design the reading experience

KUMIHAN｜Szyk doesn't dictate a finished look. It gives AI the knowledge to adapt to each medium and brief.
