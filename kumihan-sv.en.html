WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜Samspel helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything. In Swedish, that starts by asking whether a text is to be read continuously, to catch the eye as a headline or ad, or to be used as a button in an interface.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Swedish content.

01 / TYPE
å ä ö

Swedish typography

Å, ä and ö are letters in their own right, not decoration. Their rings and dots must not be clipped by tight line spacing, cramped headings or image masks, and capitals keep their accents: idé becomes IDÉ. Long compounds are never split with spaces, and when nattåg is hyphenated it becomes natt-tåg—a break a soft hyphen cannot express, so it is used only where the renderer supports it.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal. Running text, headlines and ads that catch the eye, and interfaces people operate are designed as different ways of reading. A standalone heading usually drops the full stop, but an ad line such as ”Plats för fler idéer.” may keep it—question and exclamation marks carry meaning.

03 / FIDELITY
” ’

Source fidelity

Protect names, quotations, numbers, dates, codes and intended meaning. Swedish quotes are ”…”, with ’…’ inside, and the full stop goes inside or outside depending on whether the quote is a full sentence. 1 234,50 kr and 25 % take a space; 9.45 and 9:45 are both correct, and EU:s is kept apart from EU-regler. Finland-Swedish usage is not an error. Swapping dom for dem or ni for du, or changing word order, are edits: recorded as proposals, never made silently.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files. Å, Ä and Ö are checked in capitals and lower case, in several weights and in one- to three-line headings, so that no ring or dot is clipped. Long compounds, 200% zoom and narrow screens are tested in real rendering; PDF search, fallback fonts and Office are checked separately, and anything unverified is marked as such.

DESIGNED FOR THE MESSAGE

Beyond arranging type
Design the reading experience

KUMIHAN｜Samspel doesn't dictate a finished look. It gives AI the knowledge to adapt to each medium and brief. There is no single “Nordic style”: Swedish design ranges from bold, multicoloured serif capitals to serif body text on municipal websites. Typefaces are compared by file version, character coverage, optical size and licence—a family with Text, Headline and Display cuts, like Ivar, is built for different roles.
