WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜Ahenk helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything. In Turkish, meaning often rides on the suffixes at the end of a word, so width and line breaks are adjusted before any text is cut.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Turkish content.

01 / TYPE
İı

Turkish typography

Keep İ/i and I/ı as two separate pairs when changing case, judge suffix apostrophes by grammatical type (Ankara’ya, Türk Dil Kurumuna, TDK’nin), and hyphenate only at syllable boundaries.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY
kâr

Source fidelity

Protect names, quotations, apostrophes, the circumflex in â, î, û (kar/kâr), meaning-bearing suffixes, and formats such as %12,5 and 12.345,67. Record changes as proposals, never silent edits.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.

DESIGNED FOR THE MESSAGE

Beyond arranging type
Design the reading experience

KUMIHAN｜Ahenk doesn't dictate a finished look. It gives AI the knowledge to adapt to each medium and brief. For Turkish, that includes checking that the dots of İ and i survive ligatures, small caps and font fallback.
