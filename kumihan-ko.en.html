OPEN-SOURCE SKILLS / KOREAN TYPOGRAPHY

Give Korean typography room to breathe

KUMIHAN｜글짜임 is an independent open-source set of skills for creating and reviewing Korean websites, documents and presentations. It considers eojeol, syllables, line breaks, spacing and information hierarchy while keeping the original content intact.

01 / THE IDEA

Not the same template for every page Let Korean content lead

Korean typography should respond to the reader, the width of the page and the voice of the brand. Even forms such as 3개 and 3 개 require contextual consideration rather than a blanket rule.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four principles for thoughtful design

Practical priorities for both production and review

01 / TYPE
가

Korean typography

Distinguish eojeol, syllables and grapheme clusters. Compare normal and keep-all against actual line widths.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Set priorities for information and action, then shape spacing and layout around them.

03 / FIDELITY
Aa/글

Source integrity

Preserve names, prices, quantities, origin, Unicode and actual payment states.

04 / REVIEW
◎

Read-only review

Identify issues without editing the files, and distinguish evidence from what remains untested.

DESIGNED FOR THE MESSAGE

Beyond placing letters Design the reading experience

KUMIHAN｜글짜임 does not impose a fixed font or template. It protects what words mean, too: a payment that is pending must not become a completed payment in the copy.
