KOREAN TYPOGRAPHY × OPEN SOURCE

Give Korean typography room to breathe.

KUMIHAN｜글짜임 is an open-source collection of guidance skills for creating and reviewing Korean-language web pages, documents, and interfaces. It helps examine line breaks, spacing and information hierarchy while preserving your source text.

01 / PRINCIPLES

Design begins with the content.

Respect Korean reading rhythm and the meaning of your source. Let the available width, audience and purpose shape the structure.

01

Line breaks with context

Distinguish eojeol, syllables and grapheme clusters. Compare normal and keep-all at real widths before adding forced breaks.

02

Preserve the source

Keep names, prices, quantities, origin, legal statements and Unicode unchanged without permission. Check facts before decoration.

03

Structure for the task

Separate the needs of web, UI and documents, and respect brand voice. No fixed font palette or mandatory visual template.

02 / IN CONTEXT

Small details need context.

3개 / 3 개

결제 대기 중

Neither 3개 nor 3 개 is automatically an error. Consider spelling guidance, brand practice and context. A pending payment must never become a completed payment in the copy.

03 / SKILLS

Four skills, chosen for your task.

01

Scope and route the work

kumihan-geuljjaim

Choose study, build, audit or refine, a medium and protected content.

02

Web pages and interfaces

kumihan-geuljjaim-web

Review width, mixed text, hierarchy and CTA behavior in the actual browser.

03

Documents and presentations

kumihan-geuljjaim-documents

Guidance for reports, slides and display copy. Native Office/PDF testing is separate.

04

Review without changing the source

kumihan-geuljjaim-review

Return findings and evidence in read-only mode. No automatic file edits.
