FRANCE / CANADA / BELGIUM / SWITZERLAND

One French language
different editorial conventions

Region and house style are chosen separately; house style is never inferred from the region. Without an explicit style, Accord only reviews and leaves the original text untouched.

FRFrance
fr-FR

The fr-fr-editorial style, when explicitly selected: narrow no-break space before ! ? ; and inside guillemets, no-break space before the colon.

CACanada
fr-CA

fr-ca-oqlf (Québec): no space or a narrow space before ! ? ; are both accepted and never flagged. No-break space before the colon and inside guillemets.

BEBelgium
fr-BE

No predefined house style. Without an editorial standard from the organization, the choice stays unresolved and France’s conventions are not applied.

CHSwitzerland
fr-CH

No predefined house style. The institution’s or publisher’s standard must be supplied; otherwise Accord reviews without imposing fr-FR usage.
