TYPOGRAPHY × PURPOSEFUL DESIGN

When language changes,so does typography

KUMIHAN is an open-source project that supports language-specific typesetting and the wider design of everything made with text. Across websites and documents, it refines information structure, typefaces, whitespace and reading flow, and rethinks the formulaic layouts and purposeless decoration so common in AI-generated work. Purposeful design for what you say—and how you say it.

OUR APPROACH

Beyond translationDesign for better reading

KUMIHAN deals with more than strings of text and line breaks. In any design where text carries information—web pages, articles, reports, slides—it considers structure, typefaces, whitespace and reading flow together.

The same hero, cards and calls to action every time; space filled with decoration for its own sake; track records shown without evidence. KUMIHAN includes creation and review rules for rethinking this kind of formulaic, AI-typical design. Rather than banning any particular look outright, it offers guidance for judging whether a design fits its content and purpose.

01Protect the sourcePreserve names, numbers and supplied wording
02Typeset for each languageRefine line breaks, punctuation, typefaces and whitespace
03Question AI defaultsRein in reflexively reused layouts and purposeless decoration
04Check the rendered resultCheck screens and readability, and flag anything unverified
