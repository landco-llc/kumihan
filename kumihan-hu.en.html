WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜Arány helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything. In Hungarian, that starts by asking whether each block is there to be read, looked at, recognized or acted on—arány, proportion, follows from that role.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Hungarian content.

01 / TYPE
Ő Ű

Hungarian typography

The long vowels ő and ű, with their double acute, are letters of their own—not ö and ü—so they are never swapped for look-alikes, and Ő and Ű keep their marks in all-caps headings, buttons and masks. Cs, sz, gy, ny and dzs are each one letter of the alphabet: CS and Cs are both correct capitals, csak sorts after cukor, and a grapheme segmenter that splits dzs into three is not counting Hungarian letters.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal. Text to be read, looked at, recognized or acted on gets its own proportions—in the research, one theatre’s article heading was four times its body text, one studio’s heading 1.4 times its description. Long compounds are not shrunk or cut to fit, and line-end hyphenation is kept apart from spelling: Összeg breaks as Ösz-szeg.

03 / FIDELITY
„» «”

Source fidelity

Protect names, quotations, numbers, dates, codes and intended meaning. Quotation marks nest as „…” outside and »…« inside; 10 000 keeps its no-break space, 12 345,67 its decimal comma and 2026. október 20. its full stops. Hungarian names put the family name first, but foreign names are not reversed. Changing word order, which can shift a sentence’s focus, or switching between familiar and formal address is an edit: recorded as a proposal, never made silently.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files. Ő and Ű are checked for clipped marks in capitals, italics and heavy weights, hyphenated words must still copy and search as written, and font support is judged file by file—in the research, NFC and NFD forms shaped identically in the four font files tested, which is no guarantee for every device or weight.

DESIGNED FOR THE MESSAGE

Beyond arranging type
Design the reading experience

KUMIHAN｜Arány doesn't dictate a finished look. It gives AI the knowledge to adapt to each medium and brief. Hungarian design has no single style: the type lineage of Miklós Kis and Lajos Kassák’s constructive covers served different ends. Typefaces are compared by file, version, coverage of Ő ő Ű ű, axes and licence—a designer’s nationality says nothing about Hungarian support.
