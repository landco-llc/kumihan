WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜Hlela helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing isiZulu content.

01 / TYPE
Aa

isiZulu typography

Protect conjunctive words and internal capitalization. Confirm hyphenation in the real language and rendering environment.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY
Aa/’

Source fidelity

Preserve source words, numbers, names, case and spacing. Any proposed edit comes with a diff.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.

DESIGNED FOR THE MESSAGE
