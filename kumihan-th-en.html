KUMIHAN｜ดุลอักษร

KUMIHAN｜ดุลอักษร is a set of open-source AI skills for creating and reviewing Thai websites, articles, reports, and presentations—with care for readability, layout, and the integrity of your original content.

WORDS · SPACE · PURPOSE
01 / THE IDEA

Not a uniform look—
the right form for each message

Every brief, audience, and message is different. KUMIHAN｜ดุลอักษร helps AI shape typography, spacing, and hierarchy around the meaning and priorities of each piece—rather than applying one template to everything.

02 / WHAT MATTERS

Four things worth getting right

Practical priorities for creating and reviewing Thai content.

01 / BREAKS
ก

Natural line composition

Evaluate candidate breaks against real width and meaning instead of forcing a new line after each phrase, and check stacked vowels and tone marks, font fallback, line spacing and clipping in actual output.

02 / STRUCTURE
▮▮▯

Purposeful structure

Shape the layout around the information, its priority, and the reader’s goal.

03 / FIDELITY
Aa/ก

Source fidelity

Preserve names, prices, quotations and Unicode sequences without silent fixes.

04 / REVIEW
◌

Read-only review

Check finished work and organize issues—without touching the files.

DESIGNED FOR THE MESSAGE

Beyond arranging type
Design the reading experience

KUMIHAN｜ดุลอักษร doesn't dictate a finished look. It gives AI the knowledge to adapt to each medium and brief.
