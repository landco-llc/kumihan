01

Typography should convey meaning, not just fit a box.

Thai script has preposed vowels, tone marks and word boundaries that need more than arbitrary character wrapping. Start with the content, available width, font behavior and reading context.

02

Four things worth getting right

01

Natural line composition

evaluate candidate breaks against real width and meaning instead of forcing a new line after each phrase.

02

Sound glyph rendering

check stacked signs, font fallback, line spacing and clipping in actual output.

03

Fidelity to the source

preserve names, prices, quotations and Unicode sequences without silent fixes.

04

Read-only review

report observed issues separately from guidance and untested concerns.
